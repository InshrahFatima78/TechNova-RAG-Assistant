TECHNOVA AI SOLUTIONS
Refund and Support Policy

This document explains the general refund and customer support policies of TechNova AI Solutions.

REFUND POLICY

Project-Based Services

For custom AI development and software projects, refund eligibility depends on the project agreement and development stage.

If a project has not started, a client may request cancellation according to the terms agreed in the project contract.

Once development work has started, refunds may depend on the amount of work already completed.

Completed Work

Payments for completed development work may not be refundable.

If a project has been delivered according to the agreed requirements, the client is responsible for reviewing the delivered work within the agreed review period.

Revision Requests

If the delivered project does not match the agreed requirements, the client may request revisions according to the original project scope.

Requests for completely new features that were not included in the original project scope may require an additional fee.

CUSTOM AI PROJECTS

Custom AI projects are developed according to the requirements agreed upon with the client.

The final scope, timeline, pricing, and revision policy should be confirmed before development begins.

SUPPORT POLICY

Standard Support

TechNova provides support for issues related to the delivered project according to the support terms agreed with the client.

Technical Issues

If a technical issue is caused by the delivered software, TechNova will review the issue and provide assistance according to the applicable support agreement.

Third-Party Services

Issues caused by third-party services, APIs, hosting providers, or external platforms may require additional investigation.

Additional Work

Requests that are outside the original project scope may be treated as new development work and may require additional charges.

SUPPORT HOURS

Monday to Friday: 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM

Saturday: 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM

Sunday: Closed

RESPONSE TIME

General support requests are normally reviewed within 1 to 2 business days.

Urgent support may be available depending on the client's support agreement.
